=== About Cats (General Facts) ===
Cats are small carnivorous mammals that have been domesticated for thousands of years, with evidence of human-cat companionship dating back over 9,000 years in
the Near East. A group of cats is called a "clowder," and a male cat is called a "tom" while a female is called a "queen." Cats spend an average of 12 to 16
hours a day sleeping, making them one of the most sleep-oriented mammals. Domestic cats have excellent night vision, being able to see at light levels roughly
six times lower than what a human needs, thanks to a reflective layer behind their retina called the tapetum lucidum. Unlike dogs, cats are obligate carnivores,
meaning their diet must consist primarily of meat to obtain essential nutrients like taurine, which they cannot synthesize on their own. A cat's purr is
produced by rapid, repetitive contractions of the laryngeal muscles and can occur at a frequency of 25 to 150 Hz, a range that some researchers believe may
promote bone healing and reduce pain.
